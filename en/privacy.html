2. Summary

Trendo processes your data only on your device. The app has no permission to access the internet and uses no servers, no user account, no advertising and no analytics or tracking services. I as the provider receive no data from you.

3. Which data is processed

From Health Connect, only with your permission: body weight (read and write), nutrition such as kcal, protein, carbohydrates and fat (read and write), calories burned (read), exercise sessions (read) and data older than 30 days (read).

Stored by the app on your device: your settings, phases and goals, voluntary profile details (first and last name, date of birth, height, sex and daily activity for estimating calorie needs), reminder times, a cache of your weight data for faster loading, the latest weekly-check values for the reminder and the last loaded values for the home screen widget (trend weight, weekly change, today's calories and target). If you place the widget on your home screen, these values are visible to anyone who sees your unlocked device.

4. Purpose

The data is used to show you your trend weight, plan and goal, maintenance calories, recommendations, charts and reminders. It is not used for anything else.

5. Legal basis

Weight, nutrition and exercise data are health data (Art. 9(1) GDPR). They are processed only on the basis of your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a), Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give by granting the permissions in Health Connect. Profile details are voluntary (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

6. Withdrawal of consent

You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future: in the app under Profile → Health Connect → Revoke access, or in Android's Health Connect settings. This does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

7. Storage period and deletion

Data stored by the app stays on your device until you clear the app data or uninstall the app. When you revoke Health Connect access, the app deletes its cache. Entries the app wrote to Health Connect are managed by Health Connect; you can delete them there at any time.

8. Recipients and disclosure

The app does not pass any data to me or to third parties. You trigger or control the following in Android yourself:

• Health Connect (Google): data the app stores there can be read by other apps according to your permissions, e.g. Google Health or your nutrition app.
• Android backup: if backup is enabled on your device, Android saves the app's settings (not the weight cache) to your Google account. Google is responsible for this; its privacy policy applies.
• Nutrition app and Google Play: the food button and the reminder open the nutrition app you chose (e.g. fddb or YAZIO) or its page on Google Play. The respective provider's privacy policy applies there.

9. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled and shown locally on your device; no push service is used. You can turn them off in the app or in Android's settings.

10. Website

These legal texts are also available at https://deiberger.github.io/trendo-legal/. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary access data such as IP address, time and browser in server log files to deliver and secure the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest in secure delivery). The transfer to the USA is based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, under which GitHub is certified. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics; I receive no access data. GitHub's privacy statement applies in addition.

11. No automated decision-making

Recommendations such as the kcal target or forecasts are calculations for your information. There is no automated decision with legal effect within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

12. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object (Art. 21 GDPR), and to withdraw your consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR). As your data stays on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app, in Health Connect or in Android's settings. For questions, contact me at the email address above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), e.g. the authority responsible for me, the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg.

13. Minimum age

The app is intended for people aged 16 and over.

14. Changes

If data processing changes, e.g. through new features, this privacy policy will be updated. The current version is always available in the app under Profile → About.
